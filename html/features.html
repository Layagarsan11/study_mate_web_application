Features

Smart Task Managment

StudyMate allows students to easily add and manage their daily study tasks. Users can create tasks with different priority levels and organize their study schedule efficiently.

Habit Tracking System

The application helps users build productive habits by tracking daily activities such as studying, reading, or practicing coding. Users can mark tasks as completed and stay consistent with their goals.

Progress Monitoring

Users can view detailed statistics and charts that show their progress over time. This helps them stay motivated and understand their study patterns better.

Secure User Authentication

The system includes Sign Up, Login, and Logout features to simulate a real-world web application. Only registered users can access the study planner.
